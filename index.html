01 / The bottleneck

The next generation of AI cannot be trained by scaling the same human processes that created the last generation.

The constraint

Intelligence is scaling faster than the data used to train it.

01

The internet is finite.

Publicly available data is increasingly exhausted, noisy, and poorly aligned with the capabilities frontier of modern models.

02

Expertise does not scale.

As models surpass human baselines, producing useful demonstrations requires increasingly scarce and expensive expert workflows.

03

Human feedback has a ceiling.

A system cannot reliably learn capabilities that humans themselves cannot evaluate, discover, or execute at scale.

02 / The architecture

Replace the labeler with an environment.

We construct closed-loop environments in which AI systems can generate research, act on it, and independently determine whether it worked.

01 / Simulator

Generate

02 / Agent

03 / The thesis
We don't want to make humans better at producing training data.

We want to build systems that produce it themselves.
